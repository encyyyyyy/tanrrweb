Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Tanrr prioritizes your privacy. The information we may collect includes your general location (for real-time UV index and sunlight intensity data), your selected skin type (to ensure safe tanning and Vitamin D calculations), and basic usage metrics to improve the application. We do not personally identify you, and health data remains on your device where possible.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly for the core functionality of the Tanrr app, including:

  • Calculating safe sunlight exposure times.
  • Estimating Vitamin D production based on your location and skin type.
  • Providing customized notifications and timers for sun exposure.

3. Requesting Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your data or revoke your consent at any time. Because most data is stored locally on your device, you can delete this by uninstalling the application or clearing the app data. For any explicit data deletion requests from our servers, please contact us through our Support page.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services (such as weather APIs or analytics tools). These providers have their own privacy policies addressing how they use such information.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
